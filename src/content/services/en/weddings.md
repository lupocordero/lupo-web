As a **wedding photographer in Mallorca**, I focus on a specific kind of wedding: intimate, small, one that puts the moment above the spectacle. If you're planning a wedding for 20-60 guests at a country estate, a civil ceremony at the town hall followed by a family lunch, or you simply want part-day coverage for the hours that actually matter, this is my territory.

Mallorca has plenty of photographers built for the huge, 200-guest, full-production wedding — that's simply not my focus. I've found that couples looking for real, unposed moments over a rigid shot list are usually the happiest with the way I work, and it tends to be exactly the kind of wedding that international couples marrying on the island are planning in the first place.

### What's included

I offer flexible packages: from 3-4 hours of coverage (ceremony + couple's session + start of the reception) up to a full 8-10 hour day (getting-ready, ceremony, couple's session, reception and first dance). Every package includes a planning session beforehand (in person or by video call) to walk through the day's timeline, full editing of the selected photos, and a private online gallery to share with family and friends.

If you need it, I can also help coordinate with other local vendors — florist, music, small-scale catering — through my network on the island, though photography always stays my main role on the day. For couples getting married on a tighter budget or a shorter trip, part-day coverage is often the smarter choice: you get every photo that actually matters without paying for hours of coverage you won't look back on.

### What a typical session looks like, step by step

1. **Getting ready.** Discreet photos of the final details: the dress, the rings, last-minute nerves — without getting in the way.
2. **Ceremony.** I move carefully to stay out of your guests' sightlines, capturing both the main moments and the crowd's reactions.
3. **Couple's session.** Between the ceremony and the reception, we carve out 20-30 minutes to step away and shoot just the two of you, making the most of whatever light is available at that point in the day.
4. **Reception and celebration.** Speeches, toasts, the first dance, and those unplanned moments that so often end up as the couple's favorite photos.

Throughout the day, I try to stay far enough in the background that guests forget there's a photographer working the room at all — that's usually when the most honest moments happen, long before anyone notices the camera and straightens up for it.

### Why choose me

I know what it means to plan a wedding from a distance, often without having set foot in Mallorca until a few days before. That's why I'm punctual without exception, I communicate clearly in three languages, and I understand the logistics of weddings with international guests — flights, accommodation, tight schedules. I won't be the part of your wedding you have to worry about.

I also know how to blend into the day rather than dominate it. I'm not interested in stopping the party for twenty minutes of forced group shots — I'd rather work quickly, stay aware of the schedule, and give you back the time to actually enjoy your own wedding. That balance between being present and staying out of the way is something couples consistently mention when they tell me what worked for them.

### Areas I cover

I cover weddings across the whole island, with particular experience at country estates near **Palma** and **Marratxí**, coastal villas in **Calvià** and **Andratx**, and seaside weddings in **Sóller** and **Deià** for couples who want the Tramuntana mountains as their backdrop. If your wedding is at a luxury real estate property with sea views, I can also add architectural-style photos of the property itself as a complement — something I combine with my [real estate photography](/en/real-estate) work.

I'm also happy to travel further inland to lesser-known venues that don't get much online exposure yet but photograph beautifully — small family-run fincas, for instance, that couples often only find through a wedding planner or word of mouth.
