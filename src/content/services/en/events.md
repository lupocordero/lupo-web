A good **event photographer in Mallorca** understands the rhythm of the day: knows when to stay discreet and when to move in, and delivers fast. I cover both intimate family events and corporate ones, with the same documentary approach I use for elopements: capturing what's actually happening.

### What's included

A pre-event call to understand the program and key moments, coverage for the booked time, full editing, and an express selection within hours of the event for social media if you need it.

### What a typical session looks like

1. **Early arrival.** I check the space and light before it starts.
2. **Discreet coverage.** Headline and spontaneous moments, without interrupting.
3. **Group photos.** Efficient, without eating into the schedule.
4. **Wrap-up and express selection.** Ready for immediate use if needed.

### Why choose me

Punctuality is non-negotiable at an event. My experience with German and international clients means I understand how central European corporate events run: tight agendas, fast turnarounds.

### Areas I cover

Hotels and villas in **Palma**, **Marratxí** and **Calvià**, and estates or locations further inland or along the west coast if your event calls for it.
