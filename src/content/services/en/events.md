A good **event photographer in Mallorca** understands the rhythm of the day: knows when to stay discreet and when to move in, and delivers fast. This is the area where I have the most experience and feel most at home — weddings, private parties, corporate events and trade fairs, all with the same documentary approach I use for my portraits and elopements: capturing what's actually happening.

### What's included

A pre-event call to understand the program and key moments, coverage for the booked time, full editing with a fast turnaround, and an express selection within hours of the event for social media if you need it.

### What a typical session looks like

1. **Early arrival.** I check the space and light before it starts.
2. **Discreet coverage.** Headline and spontaneous moments, without interrupting.
3. **Group photos.** Efficient, without eating into the schedule.
4. **Wrap-up and express selection.** Ready for immediate use if needed.

### Why choose me

Punctuality is non-negotiable at an event, and editing doesn't drag on for weeks: my workflow is built so you get your photos fast, usually within a day or two. My experience with German and international clients means I understand how central European events run: tight agendas, fast turnarounds.
