A good **event photographer in Mallorca** doesn't just show up with a camera — they understand the rhythm of the event, know when to stay discreet and when to move in, and deliver the photos with the speed you need, especially if they're for social media or press. I cover both intimate family events and larger-scale corporate ones, always with the same documentary approach I bring to weddings and elopements: capturing what's actually happening, not forcing staged poses.

Every event has its own rhythm and its own non-negotiable moments, and the first thing I do is figure out exactly what those are for your event so nothing important gets missed. Whether it's a milestone birthday, a company's annual kick-off, or a private celebration that only happens once, the brief is the same: be there, stay out of the way, and come away with photos that actually tell the story of the day.

### What's included

Every booking includes a pre-event call to understand the program and the moments you absolutely can't afford to miss (the speech, the cake-cutting, the signing of the agreement, the opening toast), photographic coverage for the booked time, full editing of the selected photos, and — if you need it — an express selection in the hours right after the event for immediate use on social media.

For corporate events, I can also coordinate with your communications or marketing team to make sure the photos cover exactly what you need for internal reporting or external publishing. For family celebrations, I'm just as happy taking direction from whoever's organizing — a parent, a sibling, an event planner — so the day runs the way you pictured it.

### What a typical session looks like, step by step

1. **Early arrival.** I usually arrive with time to spare before the event starts, to check out the space, available light, and coordinate with organizers or officiants.
2. **Discreet coverage during the event.** I move among guests without interrupting, capturing both the headline moments (speeches, toasts) and the spontaneous ones (laughter, conversations, reactions).
3. **Group or corporate photos.** If the event calls for it, I organize efficient group shots that don't eat too much into the schedule.
4. **Wrap-up and express selection.** Before I leave, if needed, I have a first quick selection ready for immediate use.

Throughout, I stay flexible to the actual flow of the day rather than a rigid shot list — events rarely run exactly on schedule, and being able to adapt on the fly is often what separates useful event coverage from a photographer just ticking boxes.

### Why choose me

Punctuality is non-negotiable at an event — there's no room to be late for a speech or a moment that only happens once. My experience working with German and international clients also means I understand how central European corporate events tend to run (strict punctuality, tight agendas, the need for fast turnarounds), something many local photographers don't prioritize in the same way.

I also keep communication simple: one point of contact, clear pricing up front, and a fast reply whenever the agenda shifts — which, with events, it usually does at least once. If your event includes speakers or guests from abroad, I can brief and communicate with them directly in English or German without needing a translator on-site.

### Areas I cover

I cover events across the whole island, most often at hotels and villas in **Palma**, **Marratxí** and **Calvià**, though I'm happy to travel to estates or locations further inland or along the west coast if your event calls for it. If your event doubles as a celebration with a more personal side — an anniversary party, a milestone birthday — I can also work in some of the more relaxed [portrait](/en/portraits) style shots alongside the standard event coverage.

For multi-day company retreats or incentive trips, I can also cover several sessions across the stay — a welcome dinner one evening, an activity day the next, a closing gala at the end — with consistent style and quick turnaround between each so your team has usable content throughout, not just after the whole trip wraps up.
