The **Portrait Tour** brings together two things: actually getting to know Palma, and coming home with photos that don't look like tourist snapshots. We walk through the corners of the city I like most — the ones a map won't show you — and at every stop I photograph you calmly, with no rush and no forced poses.

### What's included

A 2 to 4 hour walk through the old town and surrounding areas, a route shaped by the light and what you're interested in seeing, photos throughout the whole walk (not just 2-3 stops), and full editing with fast delivery. Great for couples, families or groups of friends up to 6 people.

### What a typical session looks like

1. **We meet at a point in the city centre** and start walking with no fixed route, letting the city set the pace.
2. **We stop at the corners, courtyards and alleys** with the best light at that time of day.
3. **We alternate candid photos** while you walk with a few moments where I'll ask you to hold still for a second.
4. **We close at a viewpoint** with the best view, usually around sunset.

### Why choose me

I live on the island and know Palma beyond the tourist route. I speak English, German and Spanish, so the session works just as well for couples, families or friends from different countries. I deliver the edited photos within a few days.

### Areas I cover

Old town and cathedral, the Santa Catalina neighbourhood, the seafront promenade and Es Baluard — we combine these depending on the light and what you feel like seeing.
