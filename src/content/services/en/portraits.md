A good portrait doesn't require faking a smile. As a **portrait photographer in Mallorca**, I let people be themselves and capture that, instead of directing a stiff session.

### What's included

Family sessions (multiple generations welcome), individual sessions, couple's portraits, and professional headshots for LinkedIn or full teams. Includes a pre-shoot consultation, the session, and editing with a carefully curated selection.

### What a typical session looks like

1. **We pick a location based on the mood.** Beach, stone village or olive grove.
2. **We start slow.** The first few minutes are about settling in.
3. **Natural activity, not posing.** Walking, playing, hugging.
4. **We close with a few directed portraits.** Some classic, posed shots if you'd like.

### Why choose me

I work with a lot of German and international families on holiday who want updated photos, and with professionals who need quick headshots. Punctuality and fast turnaround come first.

### Areas I cover

Beaches in **Palma** and **Calvià**, the rural surroundings of **Marratxí**, and the stone villages around **Sóller**. For corporate headshots, I travel to offices anywhere on the island.
