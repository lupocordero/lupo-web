A good portrait doesn't require faking a smile you don't feel. As a **portrait photographer in Mallorca**, I work with families, couples, children and professionals under the same philosophy I use for weddings and elopements: let people be themselves, and capture that, instead of directing a stiff session in front of an artificial backdrop.

Most of my portrait clients are visiting the island — on holiday, on a work trip, or back to see family — and want a set of genuinely good photos from their time here without the stress of a formal studio session. Others live locally and simply want current, honest photos of their family or themselves, something that's easy to keep putting off year after year until suddenly a decade has gone by.

### What's included

I offer family portrait sessions (including multiple generations if you need it), individual sessions, couple's portraits for anniversaries or simply to have current photos together, and professional headshots for LinkedIn, company websites or full teams. Every session includes a pre-shoot consultation on location and style, the shoot itself, and full editing with a carefully curated selection of the best images.

For families with a wide age range — grandparents, parents, toddlers, teenagers who'd rather be anywhere else — I plan the session to keep everyone comfortable and engaged, rather than expecting a single pose to work for the whole group.

### What a typical session looks like, step by step

1. **We pick a location based on the mood you're after.** A beach for something relaxed and bright, a stone village like Sóller or Deià for more character, or an olive grove for a more natural, secluded feel.
2. **We start slow.** The first few minutes are about everyone feeling comfortable — especially important with children or anyone not used to being photographed.
3. **Natural activity, not posing.** Walking, playing, hugging — I let the real interaction between you guide the photos, only stepping in to adjust light or composition.
4. **We close with a few more directed portraits.** At the end, if you'd like, we do some classic, posed shots too, so you have that option in the final gallery as well.

For headshots, the structure is a little different — we usually shoot a series of consistent poses and expressions against a clean background in under half an hour, giving you a small set of interchangeable options rather than a single fixed shot.
