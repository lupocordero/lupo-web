Food sells with the eyes before it sells with the palate, and in a restaurant market as crowded as Mallorca's, mediocre photos can make an excellent dish go completely unnoticed. As a **food photographer in Mallorca**, I work with restaurants, chefs and food projects who want their dishes to look exactly as good as they taste — with natural light, clean compositions and the same bright, honest style that runs through the rest of my work.

This applies just as much to a Michelin-track kitchen as it does to a family-run tapas bar — the goal is never to make food look artificial or over-styled, but to show it exactly as appetizing as it is when it lands on the table.

Whether you're refreshing a menu that hasn't been updated in years, building a following for a new concept, or need clean product shots for packaging and a website, the goal is the same: photos that make people want to book a table or order the product, without looking artificially staged or over-edited.

### What's included

Every session includes a pre-shoot consultation to understand what you need (a full menu refresh, social media content, product photography, or a shoot of the venue's atmosphere), the photo shoot itself using natural light wherever possible, light plating direction alongside the chef, and full editing delivered in formats ready to use for both print and digital.

For ongoing needs — a restaurant that wants a rotating library of seasonal dishes, or a producer launching new products through the year — I also offer recurring sessions at a set rate, so you always have fresh, on-brand photography ready to go.

### What a typical session looks like, step by step

1. **Planning ahead.** We go through together which dishes to shoot and in what order, prioritizing anything that's best at its peak (ice cream, hot dishes) either first or last as needed.
2. **Setting up the light.** I find the best table or corner of the venue for the natural light available at that time, or bring additional lighting if the space calls for it.
3. **Shooting dish by dish.** I coordinate with the kitchen so each dish arrives at its best moment, with quick styling adjustments between shots.
4. **Atmosphere shots.** If it's part of the brief, we finish with photos of the venue, the team, or details that help tell the brand's story.

Most sessions run for half a day, timed either around a quiet service window or a dedicated shoot slot outside opening hours — whichever disrupts the kitchen's routine the least.

### Why choose me

I work fast — I know that time in a kitchen is precious, and you can't afford a session that drags on for hours and disrupts service. I also deliver quickly, which matters a lot if you need to update your menu or social media ahead of a specific date (an opening, peak season, a press event).

Communication stays simple throughout, in English, German or Spanish depending on your team, and I'm used to working around service hours so the shoot never gets in the way of a busy lunch or dinner rush.

I also bring a photographer's eye rather than a purely commercial one to these sessions — the same instinct for natural light and honest composition that runs through my wedding and portrait work, applied to a plate of food instead of a couple or a family.

### Areas I cover

I work with restaurants and food projects in **Palma**, the **Sóller** area, and beach and hotel venues in **Calvià**, and I'm happy to travel to estates or wineries further inland if the project calls for it. If your venue also hosts private dinners or celebrations, food photography pairs naturally with [event coverage](/en/events) of the same evening.

Seasonal producers — olive oil mills, small vineyards, cheese makers — are also a regular part of my work, often around harvest time when there's genuine activity to photograph alongside the finished product shots.

If you have an upcoming opening, a press event, or a seasonal menu launch, the sooner we get a date in the diary the better — I always try to leave enough runway before the deadline for a reshoot of anything that doesn't turn out quite right the first time.
