Andratx, right at the southwest tip of the island, shifts character depending on the corner: **Port d'Andratx** with its marina and upscale restaurants, **Sant Elm** looking out at the island of Sa Dragonera, and the inland town of Andratx itself, quieter and with a weekly market. As a **photographer in Andratx**, it's an area I visit often — it's home to one of Mallorca's most established German communities outside Palma, and <span class="highlight">I work here comfortably in both German and Spanish</span>.

### The kind of sessions I do in Andratx

Weddings and private celebrations at sea-view villas and harborside restaurants, family portraits along the Sant Elm promenade or at the nearby coves, and corporate dinners or gatherings at venues right on the marina.

### Iconic spots for photos in Andratx

The marina at **Port d'Andratx**, with its yachts and restaurants stacked up the hillside, is the area's most requested backdrop — especially at sunset. **Sant Elm**, facing Sa Dragonera, offers one of the island's best horizons for a couple's or family session. **Camp de Mar**, with its small islet and wooden footbridge, is another highly photogenic classic. And the town of Andratx itself, with its stone streets and Wednesday market, gives photos a more authentic, less touristy feel.
