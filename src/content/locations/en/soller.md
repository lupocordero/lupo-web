Sóller is probably the location I'm asked for most after Palma, and it's no coincidence: as a **photographer in Sóller**, I get to work in a valley ringed by mountains, covered in orange groves, with a village of Modernist architecture and a natural harbor just minutes away. Few areas of Mallorca offer this much variety in such a small radius.

It's also close enough to Palma — about 30 minutes by car through the Sóller tunnel, or a slower, more scenic drive over the mountain pass — that it works well both as a standalone destination and as a natural add-on to a session that starts elsewhere on the island.

### The kind of sessions I do in Sóller

Sóller is, above all, my favorite spot for **elopements and intimate couple sessions** — the paths through the orange groves, the stone terraces overlooking the mountains and the valley's hidden corners are perfect for small ceremonies. It's also a highly requested setting for **weddings** at the area's many country estates, **family portraits** strolling through town or the harbor, and occasionally **real estate** for the traditional stone farmhouses being sold or rented in the area.

### Iconic spots for photos in Sóller

**Plaça de la Constitució**, with its Modernist church, is the classic starting point. The **paths through the orange groves** on the edge of town offer beautiful dappled light, especially in spring when the trees are in bloom. **Port de Sóller**, connected by a historic tram, brings a completely different mood — more maritime and relaxed. And for those after something more dramatic, the mountain roads toward **Puig Major** offer spectacular viewpoints over the whole valley.

### How I work in Sóller

Since it's an area I visit often, I know exactly which paths catch the best light at which hour, where to park without hassle, and how to plan a route that combines several backdrops without wasting time on the road. For elopements, I usually recommend early morning (fewer tourists, soft light) or the last two hours before sunset.

I also keep half an eye on the tram and bus timetables when we're shooting near the plaza or the port, since both bring a wave of day-trippers at fairly predictable times — easy to work around once you know the pattern, frustrating if you don't.

### Best time of year for photos in Sóller

Spring, especially late March into April, is the standout season here — orange blossom in the air and mild temperatures make for genuinely magical sessions. Autumn is a close second, with the harvest turning the groves gold and orange and far fewer visitors around than in summer. Summer works too, though I plan around the heat with earlier start times, and even winter has appeal for couples who like the idea of a quieter, cooler mountain setting.

A German couple who married at an estate on the outskirts of town with a 20-guest ceremony among the orange trees, followed by a couple's session at the harbor at sunset. A family who used their holiday week for a portrait session walking from the square to the tram. A real estate agency also brought me in to photograph a restored stone farmhouse just outside the village, timed for the spring blossom so the listing photos would show the property surrounded by flowering orange trees rather than the plainer look it has later in the year.
