Palma is where I'm based and one of the areas I work in most often as a **photographer in Palma de Mallorca** — not just because I live nearby, but because the city offers a combination that's hard to match: centuries of character in its historic center, Mediterranean light right by the sea, and the infrastructure of a capital city for weddings, events and high-end real estate sessions.

It's also the easiest place on the island to reach — a short drive from the airport, well connected to the rest of Mallorca, and full of hotels and rental apartments, which makes it a natural base for visiting couples and families who want to combine a session here with day trips to Sóller, Deià or the beaches further along the coast.

### The kind of sessions I do in Palma

I cover practically all of my services in the city: **weddings and elopements** at estates on the outskirts or right in the historic center for couples who want the Cathedral as their backdrop; **family portraits** in the old town or along the seafront; **real estate** for the penthouses, renovated apartments and villas in residential areas like Portixol, Son Vida or Santa Catalina; **corporate events** at the city's many hotels and venues; and **food photography** for Palma's thriving restaurant scene, from traditional spots to contemporary chef-driven kitchens.

### Iconic spots for photos in Palma

The **Cathedral of Mallorca (La Seu)** and Parc de la Mar are, without question, the most requested backdrop — especially at sunset, when the stone turns gold. The **La Lonja and Calatrava** neighborhood, with its narrow streets and old facades, offers a very different feel — more intimate, more textured. The **seafront promenade and Club de Mar** work well for sessions with the sea as the main character, and the **Santa Catalina** neighborhood, with its colorful buildings and bohemian vibe, is an increasingly popular choice for portraits and brand content.

### How I work in Palma

Since it's my home base, logistics in Palma are especially smooth: I can suggest multiple locations within a single session without long travel times, react fast if the plan changes, and know exactly when each corner of the city has fewer tourists and better light. As always, I show up on time and deliver fast — for sessions in Palma, I can often confirm availability within hours, even for last-minute requests from couples who only realize they want photos a day or two into their trip.

Because Palma is compact, it's also easy to combine two very different backdrops in one session — the old town for texture and history, then the seafront or a beach for something brighter and more open — without spending half the booking stuck in traffic between locations.

### Best time of year for photos in Palma

Palma works well almost year-round, which is part of why it stays busy for me all twelve months. Spring and autumn bring the most comfortable temperatures for walking sessions through the old town, while summer evenings offer long, warm light right up until sunset over the bay. Even winter has its charm here — quieter streets, softer light, and a city that feels distinctly local once the peak tourist season has passed.
