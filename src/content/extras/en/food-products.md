Alongside events, portraits and elopements, I also shoot <span class="highlight">product and food photography</span> in Mallorca: dishes, drinks, packaging and brand details for restaurants, hotels and small producers on the island. This work mostly grew out of my relationship with the local food scene — covering their events, and then helping with their everyday photography needs.

### What's included

Menu photography for restaurants, product sessions for food and drink brands, social content, and one-off shoots for launches. I work with natural light whenever possible, and a simple continuous-light setup when the space calls for it.

### Style

I lean toward a natural, appetising style rather than an overly styled production — clean backgrounds, real textures, light post-processing. The goal is for the dish or product to look like itself.
