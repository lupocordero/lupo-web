I also shoot <span class="highlight">property and interior photography</span> in Mallorca: homes, holiday rentals and workspaces for real estate agencies, private owners and the occasional architecture or interior design project. The goal is always the same — making the space look bright, tidy and true to life.

### What's included

A full interior and exterior shoot of the property, with special attention to natural light and framing that helps show the layout clearly. Useful both for sale or rental listings and for holiday-rental marketing material.

### How I work

I visit the property in daylight whenever possible, tidy each room lightly before shooting if needed, and deliver an edited selection that prioritises clarity and a sense of space over artificial effects.
