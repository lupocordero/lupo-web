I mostly work with German and international couples, families and companies. I speak English, German and Spanish, I'm punctual, and I deliver fast.

I work across the island, most often around Palma, Sóller, Deià, Calvià and Andratx.

## Wedding & event photography in Mallorca

Weddings, private parties, corporate events and trade fairs: this is the area where I have the most experience and where I can deliver your photos fastest. I cover everything from getting-ready to the last dance, or just the key moments, with the same documentary approach I use for portraits and elopements — capturing what's actually happening, no forced poses.

## Family portraits in natural light

For **family portraits**, I skip the studio and the forced poses. I prefer natural light, the outdoors, and enough time for kids and adults to forget the camera is there. It works just as well for a family spending a few weeks on the island as for anyone who lives here year-round and wants an honest record of this stage, with no artifice.

## Elopements in Mallorca

Sóller and Deià are my favourite ground for an **elopement in Mallorca**: cliffs, hidden coves and stone villages just minutes apart. I treat the session as something to accompany, not stage — planning the light, suggesting locations by season, and letting the ceremony, symbolic or legal, set the pace.
