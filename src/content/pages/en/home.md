I'm Lupo. I live on Mallorca and do two very specific things: shoot **elopements** — intimate ceremonies, no guest list, no protocol — and take **portraits** of families that don't feel like a studio session. Everything else grows out of that: a real wedding later, lunch on the table. I don't sell it separately — it just happens.

I also shoot **events** when they fit the same approach, and run a separate line of work for agencies and owners: **real estate photography**.

I mostly work with German and international couples and families. I speak English, German and Spanish, I'm punctual, and I deliver fast — usually within a few days.

I work across the island, most often around Sóller, Deià, Calvià and Palma.

## Elopement photography in Mallorca

Sóller and Deià are my favourite ground for an **elopement in Mallorca**: cliffs, hidden coves and stone villages just minutes apart. I treat the session as something to accompany, not stage — planning the light, suggesting locations by season, and letting the ceremony, symbolic or legal, set the pace. The goal stays the same: photos that feel like you, not a performance.

## Family portraits in natural light

For **family portraits**, I skip the studio and the forced poses. I prefer natural light, the outdoors, and enough time for kids and adults to forget the camera is there. It works just as well for a family spending a few weeks on the island as for anyone who lives here year-round and wants an honest record of this stage, with no artifice.

## Why couples and families choose me

I coordinate everything remotely, in whichever language you prefer, and deliver fast — I know the moment matters, and you shouldn't wait weeks to see it. I live on the island, know the light in every area through every season, and work only with people who fit this approach: calm, honesty, and no artificial direction.
